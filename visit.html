Before You Go

Know before you go.

A few simple tips will help you make the most of your visit.

What to Bring

Must-Haves
  • Water, especially during summer
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Sun protection
  • Bug spray
Nice to Have
  • Binoculars for birdwatching
  • Camera
  • Picnic lunch or snacks
  • Fishing gear (or borrow ours, email ahead)

Restrooms

There are no public restrooms within Willow Waterhole. Plan ahead before arriving. The nearby Dr. Shannon Walker Neighborhood Library offers indoor restrooms and is just minutes from the main entrance during normal operating hours.

Accessibility

About 8 miles of paved, ADA-accessible trails connect all six lakes. The Westbury Lake Fishing Pier and Prairie Boardwalk are also accessible.

Good to Know

  • Park hours: Dawn to Dusk
  • Dogs welcome on leash
  • Free parking throughout the park
  • Please help keep the park clean
Seasonal Visitor Guide

What to expect right now.

A quick seasonal check-in to help you plan today's visit.

Summer
July – August

Hot days, cooler mornings, and peaceful lakes.

Start early or stay late to enjoy cooler temperatures, active wildlife, and longer summer evenings.

  • Willow and Heron Lakes are excellent for wading birds.
  • Native grasses and wildflowers bring the Prairie Boardwalk to life.
  • Longer daylight hours give you more time to explore.
  • Tree-lined trails provide welcome shade.
  • Bring water, especially on warmer days.
Where to Start

Which trailhead should you use?

Each trailhead offers a different way to experience Willow Waterhole.

Westbury Lake

Trailhead: Dryad Drive – Main Entrance Best for first-time visitors

The perfect introduction to Willow Waterhole, with beautiful lake views, Turtle Bridge, and trails along the water's edge.

Parking 5300 Dryad Drive
Directions

Willow Lake & Wetlands

Trailhead: Clematis Lane Best for birding & wildlife

Start here for Willow Lake, Heron Lake, the Audubon Trail, and the park's best wetlands and birdwatching.

Parking 11200 block of Clematis Lane
Directions

Prairie Boardwalk

Trailhead: Gasmer Drive Best for prairie & nature walks

Closest to the Prairie Boardwalk, Native Prairie Preserve, and the Naturescape wood sculptures and picnic area.

Parking 4700 block of Gasmer Drive
Directions

While You're Here

A few things worth knowing.

Help keep Willow Waterhole beautiful for everyone.

Wildlife First

Watch wildlife from a respectful distance and avoid disturbing nests, habitats, and other natural areas.

Stay on the Trails

Help protect native prairie and wetland habitats by staying on established trails and boardwalks.

Leave No Trace

Please pack out everything you bring with you and help keep the park clean for the next visitor.

Dogs on Leash

Dogs are welcome throughout the park. Please keep them leashed and use the waste stations provided.

Share the Trail

Walkers, runners, cyclists, photographers, and wildlife all share the trails. Be courteous and announce when passing.

Turtle Bridge Tip

If feeding turtles at Turtle Bridge, offer corn or peas only, never bread.
