Get Involved

Powered by the people who love it.

Willow Waterhole thrives because of neighbors who give their time, their support, and their care.

Volunteer

Our volunteers are the heart of everything.

From preserving the beauty of the Greenway to hosting community events, our volunteers make it all possible. We partner with neighborhood civic clubs, scout troops, garden clubs, and passionate individuals.

  • Individuals: lend a hand at a workday whenever it suits you
  • Groups: schools, scouts, churches, and clubs welcome
  • Corporate teams: build morale while giving back

Volunteer waiver: every volunteer signs a simple waiver before their first workday. You can complete it on-site when you arrive, or email us to receive it in advance.

Membership

Friends of Willow Waterhole.

Membership is one of the simplest ways to support Willow Waterhole Greenspace Conservancy's year-round work at Willow Waterhole. Your membership helps care for habitat, trails and trees, support community programs, and welcome thousands of visitors each year.

Members receive access to members-only guided tours and birdwatching outings, priority invitations to community and conservation events, and regular updates on Greenway projects.

Other Ways to Help

Every skill has a place here.

Scout projects

More than 80 Eagle and Gold Scout projects have shaped the Greenway, from benches and birdhouses to whole trail features. Bring us your idea.

Lead or teach

Birders, anglers, gardeners, photographers: share what you know at a walk, clinic, or community event.

Local businesses

Our Local Business Partners Program connects mission-aligned businesses with the Conservancy through visibility, member benefits, and community impact. Start the conversation.

Spread the word

Follow, share, and tag #willowwaterhole, because every new visitor who falls for this place makes it stronger.
