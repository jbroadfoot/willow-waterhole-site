Flora & Fauna

Life at the Waterhole.

Meet the plants and wildlife that make Willow Waterhole one of Houston’s most remarkable urban ecosystems.

Birds & Wildlife

Osprey

If you hear a loud, musical chirping along the lakes, it is probably an osprey — one of the Greenway’s most striking regular visitors. These remarkable birds hunt by hovering over the water and plunging feet-first to catch fish with their powerful talons.

Native Plants

Cottonwood Tree

Cottonwoods grow up to 80 feet tall and turn a brilliant yellow each fall. Only female trees produce the familiar cotton-like seeds that drift through the air each spring. Their roots stabilize banks, their shade cools the water, and their leaves enrich the soil.

Aquatic Life

Freshwater Mussels

Quietly resting in the sediment of our lakes, freshwater mussels filter algae, recycle nutrients, and create habitat for invertebrates and small fish. A single adult can filter several gallons of water each day — and their presence signals a healthy ecosystem.
