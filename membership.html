Become a member.

Be part of what keeps Willow Waterhole thriving, all year, every year.

What Your Membership Supports

Year-round care, funded by neighbors.

Membership is one of the simplest ways to support Willow Waterhole Greenspace Conservancy's work across the Greenway.

Protect native habitat

Restore prairie, wetland, and woodland, and care for the plants and wildlife that depend on them.

Care for trails and trees

Maintain about eight miles of ADA-accessible trails, plant native trees, and keep gathering places in good repair.

Support community programs

Fund guided walks, birding events, volunteer workdays, and educational programming for schools and scouts.

Keep the Greenway welcoming

Help welcome thousands of visitors each year to 291 acres that stay free and open to everyone.

Member Benefits

Friends of Willow Waterhole.

  • Members-only guided tours and birdwatching outings
  • Priority invitations to community and conservation events
  • Regular updates on Greenway projects, improvements, and volunteer opportunities
