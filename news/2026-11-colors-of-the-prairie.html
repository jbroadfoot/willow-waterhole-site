Coming This Fall · November 7, 2026

Colors of the Prairie: A New Tradition Begins

This fall, Willow Waterhole hosts the inaugural Colors of the Prairie, a free chalk art festival celebrating the beauty of our native prairies, wetlands, birds, and wildlife.

Inspired by the landscapes that make the Greenway unique, the festival brings together art, nature, and community for a day of creativity and discovery. Artists will transform sidewalks into colorful murals while visitors explore educational exhibits, meet local conservation organizations, and enjoy activities for all ages.

What to Expect

The festival will feature:

  • Chalk artists creating large-scale nature-inspired murals
  • Nature and conservation education exhibits
  • Native plant giveaways
  • Hands-on ecology activities for all ages
  • Food vendors
  • Live music
  • A dedicated Kids’ Chalk Zone

Designed to become an annual tradition, Colors of the Prairie celebrates the natural beauty of Willow Waterhole while creating new opportunities for people to connect with the park and one another.
