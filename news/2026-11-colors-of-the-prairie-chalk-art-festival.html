Colors of the Prairie: Chalk Art Festival

This November, Willow Waterhole invites the community to see the Greenway in a whole new way. For one day, our concrete trails become an outdoor gallery, as local and regional chalk artists transform the pavement into a sprawling, colorful celebration of creativity, one panel at a time.

Colors of the Prairie brings together working artists, first-time doodlers, and everyone in between. Expect large-scale chalk murals inspired by the park's prairie landscape and wildlife, hands-on activity stations for kids, and a relaxed, festival atmosphere along the trail.

What to expect

The full schedule is still coming together, but the day will include:

  • Live chalk art demonstrations from featured artists
  • Family-friendly activity stations, no artistic experience required
  • Volunteer opportunities before and during the event
  • Food, music, and a chance to see the Greenway from a different angle
