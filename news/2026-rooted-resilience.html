Rooted Resilience: A Night of Stories

In Houston, we don’t have to imagine extreme weather. We’ve lived it. Flooding, heat, storms. Most of us have a story, even if we don’t always think of it that way.

On August 29, Willow Waterhole hosts Rooted Resilience, a storytelling evening centered on those experiences and how a changing environment shows up in our everyday lives. The event will be held at Drop of the Creator.

The format is straightforward. A few invited guest storytellers will share short, personal stories, along with community members who want to do the same. This isn’t a performance. It’s simply people sharing something they’ve lived through, and a room willing to listen.

Some stories will be about storms or flooding. Others might be about heat, change over time, or a moment when something shifted. Not every story has to be dramatic. The quieter ones are often the most memorable.

A writing table will be set up at the beginning of the evening with prompts for anyone who wants help getting started. You don’t have to share, but you might find that you want to.
